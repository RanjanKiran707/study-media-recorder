Reviewing this mockup

  • Everything is clickable. Data is fake and resets on reload.
  • Folder: tap a recording to play it; the whole folder becomes the queue. The sort icon (top right) turns on rearrange mode. Then drag the ☰ handle; it works with mouse and touch.
  • Record: the camera fills the screen. Tap the picture to hide or show the controls. While recording they hide by themselves after 3 s. Audio keeps the controls on screen. Done is a tick.
  • Player: the video fills the screen. Tap it to hide or show the controls (they hide after 3 s while a video plays). Audio keeps them on screen. The list icon opens Up next. A mini player stays at the bottom while you browse. Playback runs ~4× faster here so you can see it advance.
